Bookshelf

An incomplete list of books I've loved and would recommend.

People ask often enough what's worth reading that it seemed easier to write it down than to keep answering badly. This is grouped by rough subject rather than by when I read it. The groupings are loose - plenty of these could sit in two or three places, and I've put each one wherever it seemed least wrong.

Being here means I'd hand it to someone, not that it's the best book on its subject. It isn't everything I've read, and I'll keep adding to it.
